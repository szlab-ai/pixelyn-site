The essentials,
at a glance.

Resolution, frame rate, color gamut and HDR playback capability — the information your Apple TV can report about the current display environment.

These are system-reported values, not measurements of your panel. The test patterns are SDR reference content. Your TV’s picture settings and Apple TV output settings affect the result.
